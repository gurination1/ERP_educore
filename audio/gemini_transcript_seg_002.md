### **Analytical Breakdown & Actionable Specifications**

#### **1. Module & Dashboard Architecture**
* **Primary System:** College ERP (EduCore) – Admissions Desk Module.
* **Layout Structure:** Modular card-based dashboard layout containing key performance indicators (KPIs) and operational filters.
* **Component Blocks:**
  * **Block 1: Probability Radar Matrix**
    * *Metric:* Percentage scale (0 to 100%).
    * *Display Logic:* Average probability score across all active student enquiries.
    * *Data Breakdown:* Total active enquiries count displayed as an oblique fraction (`[Percentage%] / [Total Active Enquiries Count]`).
  * **Block 2: Active Counselors Metric**
    * *Metric:* Total count of active admission counselors currently operational on the desk.
  * **Block 3: Attentive Required / Exception Tracker**
    * *Sub-Block 3A (Orphaned Leads):* Enquiries assigned to counselors who have left the organization, are on leave, or whose profiles are deactivated. Tracks active leads requiring manual re-assignment.
    * *Sub-Block 3B (Stale Leads):* Enquiries with zero status updates or activity for $>5$ days.
    * *Sub-Block 3C (High-Probability Cancellations):* High-probability leads (determined via dynamic threshold settings) that were marked closed, deactivated, or cancelled. Requires audit via remark logs.
  * **Block 4: Unique Enquiries Overview**
    * Filters duplicates across multiple operational streams to provide consolidated unique enquiry counts.

---

#### **2. Search Results & Data Table Specifications**
* **Interaction Flow:** Clicking any metric block opens an expanded, persistent search-result tab/panel that replaces or overlays the current view.
* **Table Column / Data Attributes (Reassignment & Exception View):**
  * Counselor Name (Current / Deactivated)
  * Enquiry Number
  * Lead Details / Basic Student Profile
  * Current Probability Score
  * Last Activity Date & Timestamp (Descending Order)
  * Remark Logs (Mandatory for cancelled/stale leads)
* **Action Buttons:**
  * **Re-Assign:** Triggers a modal/dropdown to transfer orphaned leads to active counselors.
  * **Re-Sign / Re-Route:** Re-assigns enquiry ownership to another active counselor.

---

#### **3. Admin Panel & Global Settings**
* **Access Level:** Restricted strictly to Administrator profiles via a dedicated settings icon/button located on the dashboard layout.
* **Configurable Rules:**
  * **Stale Threshold Days:** Define the maximum allowable gap (e.g., 5 days, 10 days) between consecutive follow-up actions before an enquiry is flagged as an exception.
  * **High-Probability Benchmark:** Set the percentage cutoff (e.g., $\ge 80\%$) that defines a "High Probability" lead.
  * **Automated Action Triggers:** Mandatory task rules for every active enquiry within specified time windows to prevent uncontacted leads.

---

#### **4. School & Department-Wise Analytics**
* **Breakdown Structure:** Grouped by School / Department (e.g., Commerce).
* **Metrics Tracked:**
  * Total Enquiries
  * High-Probability Lead Count
  * Low-Probability Lead Count
  * Cancelled / Deactivated Count
  * Converted Admissions Count
* **Counselor-Wise Performance Table:**
  * Active Enquiries Handled
  * Total Closed Enquiries
  * Total Registered / Converted Students
  * Categorization: Active vs. Deactivated Counselors.

---

#### **5. Detailed Student Enquiry Profile View**
* **Trigger:** Clicking any individual enquiry record anywhere in the system.
* **Layout Tabs:**
  * **Tab 1: Student Profile & Basics:** Photo, Name, Core Contact Details, Primary Metadata.
  * **Tab 2: Course Details:** Target Course selection, Fee Structure mapping.
  * **Tab 3: Additional Information:** Supplementary candidate metadata.
  * **Tab 4: Activity Track / Tracker:** Chronological audit trail sorted in **Descending Order** (Latest activity on top). Captures dates, timestamps, communication logs (Calls made, Emails sent), and historical probability scores.

---

#### **6. Status Update & Admission Conversion Workflow**
* **Status Update Action Box:** Dropdown/Radio options:
  1. *Active Enquiry*
  2. *Enquiry Closed / Cancelled*
  3. *Admission Conversion (Proceed to Admission Form)*
* **Conversion Pipeline Rule:**
  * Selecting "Admission" routes the candidate to the **Admission Form Module**.
  * **Fee Prerequisite Rule:** The candidate must first clear the registration/admission fee payment step.
  * **Fee Verification Integration:** 
    * Fees counter receipts map directly to the corresponding enquiry number.
    * System displays receipt status flags (*Checked, Verified, Fit*).
    * Clicking the receipt verifies transaction data and populates accounting metrics into the final admission ledger.
