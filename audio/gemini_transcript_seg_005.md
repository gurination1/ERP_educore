### Analytical Breakdown

#### 1. UI Architecture & Navigation Hierarchy
*   **Settings Master Menu (Global Hub):**
    *   Course Name management (CRUD operations).
    *   Subject Name management (Course-dependent mapping).
    *   Building infrastructure master table.
    *   Approver setup configurations for courses and dynamic entities.
    *   Dialer/Enquiry integrations.
*   **Course Management Module:**
    *   Master table configuration for courses and batches.
    *   Automatic generation of Course ID, Batch Name, and Batch ID.
    *   Dual configuration options: Batch generation vs. New Course Master setup.
    *   Course properties: Full Name, Short Name, Duration, System type (Annual vs. Semester).
    *   Searchable drop-downs for course and batch lists.
*   **Fees Structure Module (`fees tab`):**
    *   Matrix-based fee layout mapped to specific courses and batches.
    *   Fee Segregation attributes: Tuition Fee, Other Fee, University Fee, etc.
    *   Category Integration: Caste category vs. Fees category.
    *   Multi-batch propagation: "Apply to other batches" search and tick feature.
    *   Dynamic Ledger Integration: Automatic tracking of credit/debit adjustments, contra entries, and running student financial records.
*   **Policies Management Module (`fees and policies` tab):**
    *   Policy creation with custom naming and fee-type bindings (Tuition, Other, University).
    *   Multiple selection toggles for fee sub-categories.
    *   Scope selector during edits: "Full Course" vs. "Current Semester".
    *   Audit trail generation: Historical record preservation via contra-entries instead of hard deletions.

#### 2. Database & Data Validation Rules
*   **Excel Upload Protocol:**
    *   Template download mechanism for bulk course and batch uploads.
    *   Automatic suppression of manually inputted system fields (Course ID, Batch ID must be left blank in Excel).
    *   Status reporting on upload failure with drop-down correction pathways.
*   **Approval Gatekeeper Rule:**
    *   *Rule:* No newly created courses, batches, or structural changes go live without explicit admin/approver sign-off.
    *   *Alert System:* PENDING FOR APPROVAL alerts route to the designated approver's dashboard with batch inspection views and a single-click approval action.
*   **Ledger Invariance & Contra-Entries:**
    *   *Financial Rule:* Zero-deletion policy for financial records. Modifying or replacing a fee policy or discount does not wipe historical rows; it generates a balancing contra-entry (debit/credit reversal) alongside the new active policy rule.
*   **Semester-Specific Fee Application:**
    *   Exclusion logic: One-time charges (e.g., Admission Fee) automatically default to zero for subsequent semesters ($0$ assignment for Semester 2+).

#### 3. Frontend Component Specifications
*   **Searchable Dropdowns:** Implemented across all master lists (courses, batches, fees, policies).
*   **Checkbox/Tick Matrix:** Used in batch fee propagation and policy cross-application.
*   **Bracketed Metadata Display:** Ledger rows display applied policies in the format: `Policy Applied: [Policy Number] - [First 10-12 Characters of Policy Name]`.
*   **Date Lock Constraint:** Transaction dates for fee posting are locked to the system day (immutable by standard entry operators).
